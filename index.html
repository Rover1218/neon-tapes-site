Neon Tapes

A new original song every week. Written, composed and produced with AI.

Neon Tapes is a personal music project built on one rule: a brand-new track, made from scratch, every single week. Late-night synth-pop, dream pop, indie, R&B, folk — the genre moves around, the schedule doesn't.

How each song is made

  • A language model writes the lyrics, tempo, key and mood.
  • An open-source music model (ACE-Step 1.5) performs the song, vocals and all.
  • The recording is levelled, given cover art, and rendered into a video.
  • The finished video is published to the channel automatically.

Nothing is sampled from existing music, and every track is original to this channel. Each upload is labelled as AI-generated, as YouTube requires.
